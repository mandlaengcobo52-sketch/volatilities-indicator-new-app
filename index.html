About the Application

Volatilities Indicator is an Android application designed to monitor synthetic indices and provide trading analysis and execution tools through direct integration with the Deriv API.

Application Features

Market Monitoring

Monitor supported synthetic indices and their live prices.

Technical Analysis

Access market structure and technical analysis information.

Master Decision

Review trade qualification and execution decisions.

Trade Management

Monitor open contracts, execution history and trade performance.

Deriv Integration

Connect a supported Deriv account through the application.

Notifications

Receive application trade and risk notifications.
